Models (optional, downloaded during installation)
-------------------------------------------------

Download location: https://github.com/FLABER87/krupno/releases/tag/models-v2

The models were converted from their authors' PyTorch weights to ONNX with
tools/convert_to_onnx.py from https://github.com/FLABER87/krupno. Files with the "_fp16"
suffix were also converted to half precision. No other changes were made to the weights.
The converted files are shared under the same licenses as the original weights.
