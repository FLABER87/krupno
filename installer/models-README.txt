Krupno upscaling models
=======================

Krupno looks for ONNX models (*.onnx) in this folder and in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Krupno\models.

Ready-made models (converted from their authors' weights, each under its own license):
https://github.com/FLABER87/krupno/releases/tag/models-v4

To convert other PyTorch models to ONNX, see tools/convert_to_onnx.py in the repository:
https://github.com/FLABER87/krupno
